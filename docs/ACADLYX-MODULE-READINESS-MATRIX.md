## Measurement rules

- **Implementation presence** means source files/routes/models exist; it is not a functional-completeness percentage.
- **Technical verification** means the relevant automated check passed on the cited SHA.
- **Production verification** requires real workflow, scope, persistence, failure/retry and operational evidence.
- A percentage is not assigned where there is no defined denominator and reproducible measurement method. "Inventory only" is not treated as 100%.

## Exit criteria

A module can be marked production-verified only after its positive and negative authorization cases, persisted-state invariants, retries/partial failures, relevant browser behavior and exact-SHA CI evidence are recorded. Until then the final decision remains **NO-GO**.
